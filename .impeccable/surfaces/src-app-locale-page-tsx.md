# Public Arcade

Scope: localized home, catalogue, and game player. Experience mode; visitors find a reviewed game and play in their browser.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Authentic playable games lead; a promotional banner does not precede the game shelves.

OWN-WORLD: User-approved Arcade Dark, solid charcoal layers, mint actions, rounded controls, large square covers, existing PNG identity, and SVG language flags.

STORY: Choose a category or game, inspect its actual information, and play. An empty catalogue remains an honest empty state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with logo, search and language controls; desktop category rail; concise opening title and filters; reviewed game cards immediately below. Rail becomes a scrolling row and cards stack on phones. Each cover leads to its game; the player exposes fullscreen and an exit action.

FORM: User-pinned Arcade Dark; code-led. No ranked candidate position, seed key, or approved image comp was produced in this session.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
