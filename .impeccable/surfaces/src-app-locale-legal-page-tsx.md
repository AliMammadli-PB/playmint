Scope: localized legal policy pages and contact/report form. Read mode for legal drafts; Operate mode for submitting a contact request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Readable initial policies and a direct contact form keep status and operator uncertainty explicit.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the public Arcade palette, shared header/footer, typography, controls and borders.

STORY: Navigate policies, read the draft, or submit a request to the existing admin queue. Supplied support/legal contacts remain real; operator name and country remain unconfirmed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared header, compact policy navigation, title and constrained reading column. Contact uses labeled fields and explicit submit feedback, wrapping to the mobile shell.

FORM: Extension of the user-approved public world; code-led. No independent candidate ranking, seed key, or image comp exists.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
