# Operator-selected linked archive review

Sources supplied by the platform operator were compared with the live catalogue on 2026-10-08. All 25 SWF files in `krestenlaust/swfgames` already map to 24 existing public games (two filenames represent Age of War 2). They were not imported again. `Electric Man 2.swf` in `flashresurrection/Swf` is another build of the existing Electricman 2; the two Breaking the Bank filenames represent one title. Revisions, Git blob identifiers and SHA-256 hashes of new candidates are pinned in `manifest.json`.

The manifest records ten candidates, not ten approved games. Only records with an operator browser review marked playable are published. Fancy Pants Adventure and The Gun Game remained empty or in preloaders in the isolated browser review; these builds must not be published until their dependency or compatibility problems are resolved. Credits and copyright remain in each SWF. The archive README's MIT claim is not treated as a licence for the original games. Publication metadata records the explicit operator request, separately from independently verified rights. Electric Box comes from Max Games' own Download SWF link; the publisher also offers game hosting on its Free Games For Your Site page.

`scripts/import-linked-swf.ts stage` creates a private, unlisted combined runtime for a real VirusTotal scan. `scan` advances that analysis (the normal scan worker also does this). `publish` requires a clean completed analysis and `work/linked-swf/reviews.json` records plus real game screenshots under `covers/`. Each accepted game gets only its own HTML wrapper, SWF and credits file. Every extracted file is hashed against its scanned bundle member before approval; the report explicitly records `scanScope: bundle-member`. The private bundle, ZIPs and source projects are removed after publication. Newly published games receive distinct, clearly labelled editorial curator profiles and mutual free follows; paid subscriptions and real-user follows are not manufactured.

Flashpoint, Internet Archive, Flash Museum and FlashArch are large preservation collections, not a finite list of missing titles or a blanket game licence. GamePix, Famobi and GameDistribution are publisher integrations with their own accounts, identifiers, ads and terms. No unauthorised account registration or third-party monetisation integration is performed by this catalogue import. Site-locked builds and dependent loaders are not patched to bypass publisher controls.
